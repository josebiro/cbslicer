THREE RHOMBI → ONE HEXAGON → END-GRAIN SLICES

Build the cube illusion from 60° stock.

Each apparent cube is three equal rhombi, each with 60° and 120° interior corners. Join their 120° corners at the center to make a long hexagonal bar, then crosscut it to board depth. Keep the light, medium and dark species in consistent positions to preserve the illusion.

For rhombus side a: altitude = a × √3/2, and horizontal offset = a/2. The long sloping edges lie at 60° to a base edge (30° to its perpendicular). These are end-view geometry angles, not universal saw-tilt settings. The component table and numbered end view below show the actual sections to make.

This recipe budgets three solid-species rhombic prisms per hexagonal source bar. Multi-layer laminations inside each rhombus are not implemented.
